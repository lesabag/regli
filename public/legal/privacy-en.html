Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Regli collects, uses, stores, and shares information when clients and independent service providers use the Regli marketplace.

Version 1.0 Effective date: June 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, login method, and language preference.
  • Profile information such as service types, short bio, profile image, and WhatsApp number.
  • Location information used for matching, booking, and service coordination.
  • Booking information including addresses, timing, recurrence settings, notes, and booking history.
  • Payment-related identifiers such as Stripe customer IDs, payment method references, and payment intent references.
  • Device information such as browser or app environment, operating system details, and basic diagnostic metadata.
  • Push notification tokens used to deliver operational notifications.

2. How We Use Information

  • To match clients and providers based on service, availability, and area.
  • To manage bookings, schedules, recurring visits, and related support operations.
  • To process and reconcile payments.
  • To provide customer support and service communications.
  • To monitor fraud, abuse, misuse, safety concerns, and payment risk.
  • To improve product quality, reliability, and marketplace operations.

3. Information Sharing

Regli shares information only as reasonably needed to operate the platform. This may include sharing data with:

  • Stripe for payment processing and payment operations.
  • Supabase infrastructure for hosting, authentication, database, and real-time platform services.
  • Other service providers required to operate the marketplace, support, security, or analytics functions.

4. Location Data

Regli may use precise or approximate location information to support address capture, nearby matching, booking logistics, and service-related workflows. You can limit location permissions through your device settings, but some parts of the service may not work fully without location access.

5. Push Notifications

Regli may send push notifications for booking updates, arrivals, payment-related updates, reminders, and account actions. Push notification tokens are stored to deliver these operational messages and may be removed when a device is disabled, signed out, or when an account is deleted.

6. Data Retention

Regli retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the marketplace, provide support, maintain platform integrity, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, process payments, and prevent fraud. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of record and applicable legal requirements.

7. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion through the app’s Settings area. When deletion is processed, Regli may delete or anonymize profile and preference data while retaining certain booking, payment, compliance, fraud, tax, or audit records where required by law or operational necessity. Historical payment and booking records may therefore remain after account deletion in an anonymized or restricted form.

8. Security

Regli uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should also protect their account credentials and devices.

9. Children's Privacy

Regli is not intended for independent use by children. Where babysitting-related information is provided, the account holder remains responsible for the lawful submission of that information and for any consents required under applicable law.

10. Policy Updates

Regli may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If material changes are made, Regli may revise the effective date and provide notice through the app or another reasonable channel.

11. Contact Information

For privacy or data questions, please contact Regli at support@regli.app.

Regli is a marketplace platform and not the employer of independent service providers. Privacy practices described here relate to Regli’s platform operations and related service providers used to operate the platform.
